Root-cause localization (from topology + self-time alone, not from
knowing the fault was injected into payment-service):
- payment-service's span is NOT explained by its own children: its only
  downstream call (to database, 13.01ms) accounts for ~2.6% of its total
  duration. The remaining ~513ms of "self time" -- time with no further
  child span to attribute it to -- sits entirely inside payment-service.
- order-service's and api-gateway's spans ARE almost fully explained by
  their children (self-time 12.79ms / 7.64ms respectively) -- their
  elevated total duration is a propagated symptom, not an independent
  contribution.
- Conclusion supported by evidence: payment-service is the root cause;
  order-service and api-gateway are downstream symptoms.
